# Image Guide

## Required Images

This folder should contain 24 images (12 genuine/replica pairs) for the game to work properly.

## Image Specifications

### Format
- **File Type**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Recommended Size**: 800x800 pixels or larger
- **Aspect Ratio**: Square or close to it works best
- **File Size**: Keep under 500KB for fast loading

### Quality Guidelines
- High resolution for detail examination
- Clear, well-lit photography
- Consistent angles between genuine and replica pairs
- Focus on the watch face and details

## Where to Find Images

### Legal Sources
- Your own photographs
- Stock photo websites with proper licensing
- Watch manufacturer press kits (with permission)
- Educational fair use resources
- Public domain watch photography

### What to Photograph
If taking your own photos:
- Use consistent lighting
- Keep the same angle for both watches
- Ensure sharp focus on dial details
- Photograph against a neutral background

## Tips for Good Comparison Images

1. **Same Model**: Ensure genuine and replica are the same watch model
2. **Similar Angle**: Take photos from the same perspective
3. **Lighting**: Use consistent lighting to make differences visible
4. **Focus on Details**: Capture dial text, hands, markers clearly
5. **Resolution**: Higher resolution helps players spot subtle differences

## Testing Without Images

The game will work without images by showing placeholders. This is useful for:
- Testing the game functionality
- Demonstrating the UI
- Development purposes

Once you add real images, the game becomes fully functional and educational!
